# 3秒クイズ形式の案 E11〜E20（内容は Cambridge Dictionary で確認済み）

| No | クイズ（問題文） | A | B | 正解 | もう一方の解説 | 例文 | 出典ページ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E11 | 「一人の時間がほとんどなかった」は？ | few moments | a few moments | A | a few は「少しある」 | She had few moments on her own. | few-little-a-few-a-little |
| E12 | 「宿題がない」の否定文で使うのは？ | some | any | B | some は主に肯定文 | He hasn't got any homework. | some-and-any |
| E13 | 2人のうち「一人ずつ」に使えるのは？ | each | every | A | every は3つ以上のまとまり | Each one takes turns cooking dinner. | each-or-every |
| E14 | 「木々の中に隠れていた」は？ | between the trees | among the trees | B | between ははっきり分かれた2つの間 | The ancient fountain was hidden among the trees. | between-or-among |
| E15 | 「映画は9時までに終わる」は？ | until 9 pm | by 9 pm | B | until はその時までずっと | The movie will be finished by 9 pm. | until |
| E16 | 正しいのは？ | informations | information | B | 数えるときは a piece of information | I found a lot of information about Ecuador. | information |
| E17 | 「いろいろあって、結局イタリアに」は？ | Finally | In the end | B | finally は苦労の末にやっと | In the end we decided to go to Italy. | finally-at-last-lastly-or-in-the-end |
| E18 | 「寒さが体調に影響した」は？ | affected | effected | A | effect は名詞「影響・結果」 | The cold weather has affected her health. | affect-or-effect |
| E19 | 正しいのは？ | an advice | some advice | B | 数えるときは a piece of advice | Let me give you some advice. | advice |
| E20 | かなり改まった「〜してもいいですか」は？ | Can I …? | May I …? | B | Can I はふだんよく使う | May I leave the room? | may |

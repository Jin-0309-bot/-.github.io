# 3秒クイズ形式の案 E41〜E50（内容は Cambridge Dictionary で確認済み）

| No | クイズ（問題文） | A | B | 正解 | もう一方の解説 | 例文 | 出典ページ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E41 | 「ニューヨークに行ったことはある？」は？ | Have you ever gone to | Have you ever been to | B | has gone to は行ってしまってまだそこにいる | Have you ever been to New York? | word-choice-go-gone-and-been |
| E42 | 「長旅で疲れた」は？ | tired of | tired from | B | tired of は「うんざり」 | I'm tired from the long journey. | word-choice-tired-of-or-tired-from |
| E43 | 「パーティーを開こうかと考えている」は？ | thinking of having | thinking to have | A | 意見を聞くときも think of / about（What do you think of …?） | We are thinking of having a party. | word-choice-think-about-think-of-or-think |
| E44 | 「試験でうまくいくと思っていた」は？ | She waited to do well | She expected to do well | B | wait は来るまで待つ | She expected to do well in the exam. | word-choice-wait-or-expect |
| E45 | 「会議に出席した」は？ | I joined a conference | I attended a conference | B | join は団体の一員になる。くだけた場面では go to | I attended a conference about global warming. | word-choice-join-or-attend |
| E46 | 「ほかの学生と知り合えるように」は？ | know | get to know | B | know はすでによく知っている | so we could get to know the other students | word-choice-know-or-get-to-know |
| E47 | 「今のところ、いい天気が続いている」は？ | until now | so far | B | until now は続いていたことがちょうど終わったとき | The weather has been lovely so far. | word-choice-until-now-or-so-far |
| E48 | 「ジムより良い」は？ | better that | better than | B | that は I think that … のように文をつなぐ | The sports club is better than the gym. | word-choice-that-or-than |
| E49 | 「ドイツ語を上手に話す」は？ | speak German good | speak German well | B | good は形容詞（a good cook） | I speak German well. | part-of-speech-good-or-well |
| E50 | 「簡単に受かるはず」は？ | pass the exam easy | pass the exam easily | B | easy は形容詞「簡単な」 | You should pass the exam easily. | part-of-speech-easy-or-easily |

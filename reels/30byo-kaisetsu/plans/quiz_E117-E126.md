|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E117 | 「5時に会おう」の「に」は？ | at five o'clock | on five o'clock | A | on は日付・曜日（on Monday） | I'll see you at five o'clock. | at-on-and-in-time |
| E118 | 「誕生日に」は？ | at your birthday | on your birthday | B | at は時刻や the weekend（英） | Happy birthday! What did you do on your birthday? | at-on-and-in-time |
| E119 | 「7月に」は？ | in July | on July | A | on は特定の日（on 25 October） | We moved here in July. | at-on-and-in-time |
| E120 | 「駅で待ってる」の「で」は？ | at the station | in the station | A（場面による、要確認） | in は建物の中にいることを強調 | I'll wait for you at the station. | at-on-and-in-place |
| E121 | 「部屋の中に入る」は？ | go in the room | go into the room | B | in は中にある状態 | She went into the room. | in-into |
